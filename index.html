[02] Human Personality & Engineering Ethos

Vibe Coding Meets Rigorous Systems Thinking

I am an Information Technology student at Devang Patel Institute of Advance Technology and Research (DEPSTAR), CHARUSAT (2025–2029), maintaining a CGPA of 8.12.

"To me, vibe coding is not about cutting corners — it's about eliminating the friction between a creative spark and a working, testable system."

My journey began with raw curiosity about how software interacts with the physical and human world. Instead of simply studying algorithms from a textbook, I build them: extracting video frames to monitor crowd densities at railway stations, aggregating authorization logs to catch insider security threats, and testing deepfake detection pipelines on real video feeds.

I believe modern engineering thrives when deep technical discipline is paired with fast, fearless experimentation. Whether collaborating in high-pressure national hackathons like FinSpark '26 (where our team achieved a Top 20 national ranking) or crafting clean standalone tools, my focus remains constant: building practical technology that solves real human problems.

Based in Vadodara, Gujarat, I am fluent in English, Hindi, and Gujarati. I am currently deepening my knowledge in high-performance neural model inference, computer vision edge pipelines, and secure cloud system architecture.

01

Pragmatism Over Hype

I prioritize real utility over buzzwords. A simple heuristic with high precision is infinitely more valuable than an uninterpretable, bloated neural black box.

02

Security & Digital Trust First

Systems must be designed defensively from frame one. User privacy, credential integrity, and ethical data boundaries are non-negotiable architectural requirements.

03

High-Velocity Iteration

I prototype quickly to validate core assumptions with real data, then systematically harden code, eliminate race conditions, and polish user experience.
