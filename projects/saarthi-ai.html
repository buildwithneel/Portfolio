Saarthi AI: Accessible AI Counselling & Student Guidance

An AI-assisted student companion platform designed to offer confidential, stigma-free academic and emotional guidance, signposting institutional mental health support when severe distress is detected.

1. The Problem

Undergraduate students in demanding technical disciplines frequently suffer from academic isolation, burnout, and career anxiety. However, stigma, fear of faculty judgment, and limited campus counsellor availability prevent the vast majority of students from seeking early help.

Students need an accessible, judgment-free space to untangle overwhelming academic stress and receive empathetic, pragmatic guidance.

2. Intended Audience

First-Year & Undergrad Students

Struggling with curriculum transitions, syllabus overload, exam anxiety, or peer comparison.

Campus Mentorship Desks

Can use anonymous triage trends to understand campus-wide stress patterns without compromising student identities.

3. Proposed Solution & Safety Guardrails

Saarthi AI was designed with strict ethical boundaries: it is not a replacement for human therapists. The conversational engine was framed around active listening, study habit coaching, and strict trigger-word detection that immediately forwards helpline resources if self-harm or clinical crisis signals emerge.

4. Neel's Role & Implementation Details

  • Designed the student-centric prompt personas ensuring non-judgmental, constructive responses.
  • Implemented keyword-level crisis intercept triggers to immediately provide verified Indian student support helplines (Tele-MANAS, Vandrevala Foundation).
  • Developed the initial conversational prototype tested during hackathon judging.
